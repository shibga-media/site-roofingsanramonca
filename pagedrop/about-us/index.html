Our Story

CJ's Roofing was born right here in San Ramon in 1995, when founder CJ recognized that homeowners in the Tri-Valley deserved a roofing contractor they could genuinely trust — one who would show up on time, communicate clearly, and treat every roof like it was their own.

What started as a small operation serving neighborhoods near Crow Canyon Road and the Iron Horse Trail has grown into one of the most recognized roofing names in the East Bay. Over the years, we have worked on everything from the classic ranch-style homes tucked into the hillsides of Bollinger Canyon to newer construction in the developments surrounding Bishop Ranch. We have replaced roofs battered by winter storms, repaired damage from the region's dry, scorching summers, and helped countless homeowners navigate the insurance process after unexpected events.

Our growth has always been driven by referrals — neighbors telling neighbors — and that word-of-mouth trust is something we protect fiercely. We have supported local schools, sponsored community events, and partnered with neighborhood organizations across San Ramon because this city is not just our market, it is our home. Twenty-nine years in, our commitment to quality and community has never wavered. Every crew member we hire shares that same sense of pride in the work we do and the people we serve.

How We Work

We know that having work done on your roof can feel disruptive and stressful. That is why we have built a process designed around clear communication, careful planning, and deep respect for your home and your time.

Step 1 — Free Consultation and Honest Assessment

Every project begins with a thorough, no-pressure inspection. We climb up, look carefully, and give you a straight answer about what your roof actually needs — nothing more, nothing less.

Step 2 — Transparent, Detailed Estimate

You receive a written estimate that breaks down materials, labor, and timeline in plain language. No vague line items. No surprise charges. You know exactly what you are agreeing to before any work begins.

Step 3 — Scheduling That Respects Your Life

We work around your schedule as much as possible and give you a realistic start date we actually keep. If anything changes, we contact you immediately — not the day of.

Step 4 — Clean, Professional Installation

Our crews arrive prepared, treat your landscaping and property with care, and follow strict safety protocols throughout every job. Quality is checked at every stage, not just at the end.

Step 5 — Final Walkthrough and Follow-Up

When the job is done, we walk the property with you, answer every question, and make sure you are completely satisfied. We also follow up after the project to confirm everything is performing as expected.
