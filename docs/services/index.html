Not sure which you need?

A leak doesn't always come from where the stain on the ceiling is. Water can run along the underlayment or the roof deck for several feet before it drips. Call or text (925) 548-0932, send a few photos if you can, and Chris will tell you what he'd look at first.

What we don't do

No metal roofing, no siding, no remodeling. If your project is one of those, we'd rather tell you now than waste your time.
